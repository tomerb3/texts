3. STARTING FILTERS - PROPOSED, NOT VALIDATED
SMA50 proximity: show signed distance = (close - SMA50) / SMA50 * 100.
Proposed candidate zone: -2% to +2% around SMA50, with above/below shown.
Being below is a rebound setup, not confirmation of regained support.
Being just above is a different setup; test and report each side separately.
Do not choose the best-looking threshold after seeing historical profits.

ATR: Wilder ATR(14), as percent of latest close.
Proposed daily ATR% zone: 1.5%-3.5%.
This is a starting research range, not evidence of profit or a safe 1% stop.
Higher ATR may make a tight stop easier to hit; lower ATR can leave less
room for a gross intraday target after costs. Daily ATR is not a prediction.

Relative volume: average share volume of the last 2 completed sessions
/ average share volume of the last 20 completed sessions, including the 2.
Proposed range: 1.0x-2.5x. Below 1.0x is below that trailing average;
above 2.5x requires reviewing the event/news rather than automatic rejection.
This daily metric is not today's time-of-day-adjusted intraday relative volume.

Company size: proposed minimum USD 10 billion market capitalization.
Add a separate proposed liquidity check: trailing 20-session average traded
dollar volume >= USD 50 million. Size is not a substitute for liquidity.
Use a timestamped current market-cap source; do not infer it from price.
If a threshold leaves fewer than five candidates, report fewer. Never pad.
